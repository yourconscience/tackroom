        Status vocabulary

        The glyph is the severity and matches the CLI: ✓ fine, ! needs a look, ✗ broken. Pinned is the one state that carries the accent, because the pin is the product.

        • ✓synced

          The agent's native config matches the repo.

        • ✓pinned a1f9c02

          An external skill is locked to this commit in tackroom.lock.

        • !held 41h of 72h
          31h left

          A new pin is younger than 72 hours. It syncs on its own when the time runs out.

        • ✓audit pass

          No findings in the pinned content.

        • !audit finding net-fetch

          A finding is open. An unaccepted critical finding becomes ✗ blocked and the previous pin stays.

        • !drifted 2 files

          Someone edited the agent's copy by hand. The next sync repairs it.

        • ✗missing skills/code-review

          Something the repo declares is not in the agent's config.
